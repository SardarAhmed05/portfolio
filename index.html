03 — ABOUT

A student who ships

I’m in my third year of a BS in Artificial Intelligence at Sindh Madressatul Islam University in Karachi — and I spend most of my time outside lectures building software that people actually use.

I started in classical machine learning, doing the whole job end to end — taking messy data, shaping it into something a model could learn from, and tuning until the results held up. It taught me patience, and it taught me how models actually fail.

What pulled me forward was a simple question: what happens when a model stops just predicting and starts doing? That led me into LLM-powered apps and agents — software that talks to people, reasons through a task, and finishes real work. These days I split my time between building those products for clients and a research internship at Alphatron, where the models have to prove themselves on real sensor data rather than clean benchmarks.

That combination is the point. I’m not only a prompt-and-prototype builder, and not only a theory student. I can build the website, the system behind it, and the AI inside it — and explain every layer of what I’ve made.
